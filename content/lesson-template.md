# Lesson title

> One-sentence promise: what will the reader understand or be able to do?

- **Type:** Lesson / Guide / Framework / Field note
- **Topic:** Product & customers / Distribution / Money / Operations / Founder decisions
- **Audience:** Who this is for
- **Reading time:** Estimate

## The problem

Describe the real founder question or situation.

## The lesson

Explain the idea clearly, with practical examples that are not presented as universal rules.

## A practical framework

Give the reader a sequence, checklist, question set, or decision rule they can apply.

## Try this

A short exercise or next action.

## Reality check

What could make this advice wrong, incomplete, or dependent on context?

## Takeaway

One memorable sentence.

---
Editorial checklist:
- Is this organized around a founder problem?
- Is the advice specific enough to act on?
- Are claims and examples grounded?
- Does it avoid hype and false certainty?
- Is the next step clear?
