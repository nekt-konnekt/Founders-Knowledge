MarketMinder

Amina Okafor · Sample founder

A lightweight stock and sales tracker designed around the way independent market traders actually work.

THE PROBLEM

Small traders can lose track of fast-moving stock, daily sales, and the cash tied up in inventory.

Lagos, Nigeria

Why this is interesting

Inventory tools often assume tidy spreadsheets and stable routines. This concept explores a simpler workflow for busy, cash-based businesses.

AVAILABILITY

Private build; not available to the public in this mockup.

WHAT NEEDS VERIFYING

Whether traders return to the tool daily, how stock is entered, and whether the workflow saves time or reduces stock-outs.

EDITORIAL NOTE

Illustrative profile only. Founder, product, and details are fictional.

KopaFlow

Brian Mwangi · Sample founder

A cash-flow companion that helps small service businesses track invoices, incoming payments, and upcoming commitments.

THE PROBLEM

Owners can be profitable on paper and still struggle to know what cash will be available next week.

Nairobi, Kenya

Why this is interesting

Cash-flow planning can be too complex for small teams that do not have a finance manager.

AVAILABILITY

Closed beta in this fictional scenario.

WHAT NEEDS VERIFYING

Payment-data reliability, repeat use, and whether forecasts improve decisions rather than simply summarize balances.

EDITORIAL NOTE

Illustrative profile only. Founder, product, and details are fictional.

PrototypeHealth

ClinicLoop

Efua Mensah · Sample founder

A simple follow-up workflow for small clinics to remind patients about check-ins and next appointments.

THE PROBLEM

Busy clinics may struggle to keep follow-up tasks visible after a patient leaves the facility.

Accra, Ghana

Why this is interesting

The value may be less about adding another clinical system and more about fitting follow-up into a team's existing routine.

AVAILABILITY

Prototype concept; no real patients or clinics involved.

WHAT NEEDS VERIFYING

Staff workflow fit, consent and privacy safeguards, reminder delivery, and follow-up completion.

EDITORIAL NOTE

Illustrative profile only. Founder, product, and details are fictional.

Routewise

Chidi Okeke · Sample founder

A dispatch planning dashboard concept for small delivery teams coordinating multiple riders and drop-offs.

THE PROBLEM

Dispatchers can spend hours coordinating routes and responding to changes through scattered messages.

Lagos, Nigeria

Why this is interesting

Last-mile efficiency depends on real constraints: traffic, failed deliveries, rider capacity, and changing customer availability.

AVAILABILITY

Shown as launched for demo purposes only; there is no real product link.

WHAT NEEDS VERIFYING

Route completion rates, failed delivery reduction, dispatcher time saved, and how the tool handles exceptions.

EDITORIAL NOTE

Illustrative profile only. Founder, product, and details are fictional.

Idea / waitlistLearning

TandaLearn

Zainab Uwimana · Sample founder

A mobile-first learning concept built around short practical lessons and small portfolio projects for early-career workers.

THE PROBLEM

People need job-relevant skills but may not have the time, bandwidth, or money for long courses.

Kigali, Rwanda

Why this is interesting

Short lessons only matter if they help people do something useful. The test is whether learners finish, apply, and can show what they learned.

AVAILABILITY

Waitlist concept only in this fictional scenario.

WHAT NEEDS VERIFYING

Completion, demonstrated skill gains, access constraints, and whether employers value the resulting work.

Illustrative profile only. Founder, product, and details are fictional.
