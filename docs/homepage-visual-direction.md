# Founders Knowledge — Homepage visual direction

Status: prototype branch for review, not yet production-approved.

## Design thesis
Combine the confident, playful editorial personality of the creative-studio reference with the clean information structure and whitespace of the tennis-programmes reference. FK remains a publication and reusable knowledge library—not an agency portfolio, generic newswire, or SaaS dashboard.

## Visual language
- Warm off-white paper foundation; deep ink typography.
- Deep FK purple as the primary brand accent, supported by orange, pink, and yellow.
- Oversized, compact editorial headlines; restrained sans-serif body copy.
- Strong rectangular editorial cards with deliberate colour blocks and thin dark borders.
- Spacious sections and clear scanning hierarchy; responsive stacked cards on mobile.
- Hero illustration retains FK's question/decision motif, recast as a vivid editorial cover composition rather than a cluster of pastel UI tiles.

## Content hierarchy
1. Brand and simple navigation.
2. A clear promise for African builders.
3. Curated knowledge organised by founder problem, not publication date.
4. A featured lesson that demonstrates the publication's point of view.
5. Editorial approach and newsletter information.

## Guardrails
- Preserve existing lesson URLs, content, analytics, structured data, and publishing workflow.
- Do not turn the homepage into a chronological news feed.
- Do not add stock photography or decorative imagery without a clear editorial purpose.
- Lesson reading pages remain unchanged in this first homepage prototype.
- Review at desktop and mobile widths before merging.
