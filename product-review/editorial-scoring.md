# Founders Knowledge editorial triage score

This is a transparent, deterministic triage aid for the private Product Discovery review queue. It does not make publishing decisions and does not claim to verify novelty.

## 100-point rubric

- **African relevance — 30 points:** Does the submission show an explicit connection to African markets, founders, users, or operating conditions? The current queue heuristic uses the submitted location, audience, description, and problem fields as a signal; a human reviewer must confirm the actual relevance.
- **Founder usefulness — 25 points:** How clearly does the submission explain the problem, target audience, and product value?
- **Evidence readiness — 20 points:** Are there credible paths to verify the product, such as a working website, demo, or meaningful product stage?
- **Specificity — 15 points:** Are the problem and product description concrete enough to investigate?
- **Verification paths — 10 points:** Are useful public links supplied for follow-up?

## Interpretation

- **75–100: High priority** — review sooner, not an automatic approval.
- **55–74: Worth reviewing** — investigate the strongest angle and fill evidence gaps.
- **0–54: Needs more evidence** — request clarification or defer.

## Editorial guardrails

1. The score is a prioritization heuristic, not an AI verdict.
2. A missing Africa keyword is not proof that a product is irrelevant to African founders.
3. Novelty cannot be scored responsibly without checking existing coverage and competing products.
4. Verify material claims independently; treat founder-provided copy as a lead, not proof.
5. Editorial approval remains human-controlled.
